# Learning Session

When this skill is used, conduct a structured teaching session.

## Step 1 — Assess

Ask what the developer already understands.

Do not assume zero knowledge.

## Step 2 — Prerequisites

Identify the concepts required before understanding the topic.

## Step 3 — Explain

Explain from first principles.

Use simple examples.

## Step 4 — Connect

Explain how the concept applies to the current project.

## Step 5 — Explore

Discuss alternatives and tradeoffs.

## Step 6 — Practice

Give the developer a small problem or implementation task.

Let them attempt it.

## Step 7 — Review

Review their answer.

Correct misconceptions.

## Step 8 — Connect to Engineering

Explain where the concept appears in real production systems.

## Step 9 — Interview Connection

When relevant, explain how this concept may appear in technical interviews.

Do not turn every session into interview preparation.

## Final Check

Ask a few questions to verify understanding.
